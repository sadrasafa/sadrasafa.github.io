Sadra Safadoust

I am a Research Engineer at UNVEST R&D Center and a final-year PhD candidate at Koç University, where I am an AI Fellow at the KUIS AI Center advised by Dr. Fatma Güney. My research is in computer vision with a focus on 3D reconstruction.

Before my PhD, I completed my Master's degree in Computer Science and Engineering at Koç University and my Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering at Sharif University of Technology.

Publications

FlowIt: Global Matching via Hierarchical Transformers and Optimal Transport for Optical FlowORAL

Sadra Safadoust, Fabio Tosi, Matteo Poggi, and Fatma Güney
BMVC 2026|British Machine Vision Conference
paper code project

A transformer-based global matching architecture for optical flow, using Optimal Transport-based flow initialization, resulting in state-of-the-art performance.

WarpRF: Multi-View Consistency for Training-Free Uncertainty Quantification and Applications in Radiance Fields

Sadra Safadoust, Fabio Tosi, Fatma Güney, and Matteo Poggi
WACV 2026|Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision
paper code project

A training-free framework for quantifying uncertainty in radiance fields, applicable to any representation (e.g., NeRF and 3D Gaussian Splatting), by warping renderings across views and measuring their consistency from an unseen viewpoint.

Self-Evolving Depth-Supervised 3D Gaussian Splatting from Rendered Stereo Pairs🏆 Best Poster Award

Sadra Safadoust, Fabio Tosi, Fatma Güney, and Matteo Poggi
BMVC 2024|British Machine Vision Conference
paper code project

A novel 3D Gaussian Splatting training framework that renders virtual stereo pairs and leverages their estimated depth to improve geometric accuracy and novel-view synthesis quality.

Multi-Object Discovery by Low-Dimensional Object Motion

Sadra Safadoust, and Fatma Güney
ICCV 2023|International Conference on Computer Vision
paper code project

A motion-supervised framework for single-image depth estimation and multi-object segmentation, trained without ground-truth labels and supervised purely by optical flow.

DepthP+P: Metric Accurate Monocular Depth Estimation using Planar and Parallax

Sadra Safadoust, and Fatma Güney
arXiv 2023|arXiv preprint
paper

A self-supervised monocular depth estimation framework that follows the traditional planar-parallax paradigm to predict metrically accurate depth, avoiding the scale ambiguity inherent in existing self-supervised approaches.

Stochastic Video Prediction with Structure and Motion

Adil Kaan Akan, Sadra Safadoust, and Fatma Güney
arXiv 2022|arXiv preprint
paper

Factorizes video into static (scene structure and ego-motion) and dynamic (independently moving object) components for better stochastic prediction in driving scenes.

Self-Supervised Monocular Scene Decomposition and Depth Estimation

Sadra Safadoust, and Fatma Güney
3DV 2021|International Conference on 3D Vision
paper project

Jointly estimates depth and segments objects that share the same motion, from monocular video without any ground-truth labels.
